    Content-value protection

    • Preserve all useful verified existing information.
    • Never shorten content merely to satisfy validation.
    • Enrich thin pages using approved product, supplier, official, category and global sources.
    • Never invent prices, timings, policies, inclusions, restrictions, capacity or guarantees.
    • Apply different, relevant tourism keyword maps to each product and prevent keyword cannibalization.
    • Write for customers, traditional search engines and AI discovery platforms.

    Factual source hierarchy

    PrioritySourcePermitted use
    1Approved product-specific informationPrimary source for operational and commercial facts.
    2Verified existing WordPress informationPreserve useful facts unless a stronger approved source corrects them.
    3Approved supplier or attraction informationEnrich inclusions, schedules, restrictions and product details.
    4Official attraction/operator websiteConfirm factual visitor information with source date and URL.
    5Approved category rulesDetermine required structure and safe non-commercial guidance.
    6Approved global company rulesApply consistent brand, booking and editorial requirements.
    7General explanatory contentImprove clarity only; never create commercial or operational facts.

    Source conflicts must be flagged internally. The agent cannot select whichever fact is easier to publish.

    Product-specific search architecture

    • One natural focus keyphrase and approved variations.
    • Four related keyphrase groups with relevant synonyms.
    • Category, location, customer-intent and booking-intent terminology.
    • Different relevant tourism vocabulary for every product.
    • Search Console evidence and internal-link opportunities where available.
    • Keyword-cannibalization checks against other products and category pages.
    • Natural placement for customers, traditional search and AI discovery—never keyword stuffing.

    Final Excellence Gate

    GateRequired result
    Content-value protectionNo useful verified information lost; thin content is enriched rather than copied or shortened.
    Factual integrityNo invented prices, schedules, policies, inclusions, restrictions, capacity or guarantees.
    Category complianceCorrect global, category and subtype structures applied in the right hierarchy.
    Human editorial qualityNatural tourism-professional writing with no AI filler, spinning, awkward synonyms or mechanical patterns.
    Product individualityNo duplicated FAQs, Why Choose lists, introductions or section wording across products.
    Search qualityIntent coverage, metadata, headings, entities and internal links are relevant and non-spammy.
    AI visibilityImportant facts and answers are clearly structured and easy to extract without unsupported claims.
    Publication safetySnapshot, approval, dry run, source hash, execution token and post-write verification all pass.

    Any failed gate returns the content to internal repair. Unsafe factual conflicts remain blocked instead of being shown as a publishable proposal.
