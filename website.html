    Global Product Content Rules

    Applied before category and product-specific rules.

    Approved discussion framework
    SectionFormatCore requirement
    Short Description2 short paragraphs; no bullets70–110 words; exact product, location, main value and natural focus keyphrase.
    Overview3–5 short paragraphs180–300 words; preserve verified information and provide a complete professional introduction.
    What to ExpectParagraphs or logical stagesExplain the real customer journey without fictional storytelling or duplicated content.
    HighlightsNormally 5–8 bulletsDistinctive product-specific value; no generic marketing filler.
    InclusionsVerified bulletsAll confirmed inclusions; no artificial minimum count and no invented package benefits.
    ExclusionsVerified bulletsClear exclusions and paid add-ons; omit rather than guess.
    Timing & DurationStructured factsSeparate operating, reporting, departure and approximate-duration information.
    Booking & PolicyBullets or factual H3 groupsApproved terms only; grammar may improve but legal and commercial meaning cannot change.
    Why ChooseNormally 5–6 bulletsCredible reasons connected to the exact product; never copy one generic list across products.
    FAQs5–8 visible H3 questionsDirect, useful, supported answers written for customers and conversational search.
    Related Experiences3–6 internal linksRelevant complementary products with natural, verified anchors.
    Human Editorial QualityMandatory excellence gateNatural, specific tourism writing with no AI filler, keyword stuffing or mechanical patterns.

    Content-value protection

    • Preserve all useful verified existing information.
    • Never shorten content merely to satisfy validation.
    • Enrich thin pages using approved product, supplier, official, category and global sources.
    • Never invent prices, timings, policies, inclusions, restrictions, capacity or guarantees.
    • Apply different, relevant tourism keyword maps to each product and prevent keyword cannibalization.
    • Write for customers, traditional search engines and AI discovery platforms.
