Permanent transport blueprint

These requirements are built into the agent and apply automatically to every product.

  • Short description with transport search intent
  • Vehicle/service overview and fit
  • Passenger and luggage details without guessing
  • Airport transfers and pickup process
  • Corporate, executive, event and hotel transport
  • Hourly chauffeur and full-day booking options
  • Private journeys and recurring enquiries
  • Inclusions, exclusions and extra charges
  • Timings, availability and booking information
  • Why choose Premier Express Tourism
  • Step-by-step quote request and WhatsApp CTA
  • At least 6 product-specific FAQs
  • Related vehicles and fleet categories

Research and quality gates

  • Use main-site product facts as a starting point; flag gaps rather than guessing.
  • Research current search intent and competitor page structures; do not copy their wording or claims.
  • Use a unique, natural focus keyphrase, synonyms and related keyphrases.
  • Include useful keyphrases in SEO title/meta and relevant headings without stuffing.
  • Check content structure, duplication, unsupported claims and conversion clarity.
  • Target content quality 100/100; block anything below 90/100.
  • SEO must score exactly 100/100 before publication is considered.
  • Human approval, separate WordPress credentials, dry run and post-publish verification remain mandatory.
